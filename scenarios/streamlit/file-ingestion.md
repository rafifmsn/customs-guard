## 4. Technical Commodity Descriptions & Functional Specifications

### Item 1: High-Performance Cellular Mobile Handsets
Item 1 comprises one hundred brand-new commercial units of Apple iPhone 15 Pro Max equipped with 256GB internal solid-state storage.
Each device features cellular radio modules supporting multi-band 5G Sub-6GHz, Wi-Fi 6E, and Bluetooth 5.3 wireless capabilities.
The commodities are packaged in secure retail boxes packed into heavy-duty export master cartons with tamper-evident security tape.
Each unit contains a unique international mobile equipment identity (IMEI) number logged on the manufacturer packing list.
These commodities require official verification against Indonesian telecommunications import restrictions (Lartas) under Ministry of Communication and Informatics regulations.

### Item 2: Clinical Ultrasonic Diagnostic Imaging Apparatus
Item 2 consists of two complete medical diagnostic ultrasonic scanning systems with multi-frequency broadband transducers and digital Doppler processing modules.
The apparatus is intended for diagnostic clinical medical imaging in hospital environments.
The systems are housed in shock-absorbing flight cases equipped with continuous temperature and vibration monitoring telemetry.
The equipment is subject to strict health product regulatory controls administered by the Ministry of Health of the Republic of Indonesia.
Prior to border clearance, the importer must ensure valid medical device distribution approval (Izin Edar Alat Kesehatan) is registered in the national customs single-window database.

### Item 3: Enterprise Computing Processing Infrastructure
Item 3 consists of five high-density enterprise server processing units designed for rackmount data center computational deployment.
Each unit features dual multi-core server processors, 512GB ECC DDR5 server memory, redundant 1200W titanium power supplies, and integrated hardware cryptographic modules.
The servers are formally categorized as commercial digital processing units without integrated monitor screens.
Under the official Indonesian customs tariff schedule, these computing processing units are classified at a 0.0% Most-Favoured-Nation customs duty rate.
If an importer erroneously declares this commodity under an arbitrary conservative rate, the financial engine must flag the discrepancy for legitimate duty restitution recovery.
